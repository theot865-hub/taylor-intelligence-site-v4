What does the purchase cover?+

The checkout collects CAD $1,500 for website design and development. We’ll talk through your business, content and requirements with you. If you need a store, custom software or a more involved integration, speak to us first so we can confirm the scope.

Maintenance and SEO are CAD $500 per month, billed separately starting when your website launches. The initial checkout does not start that monthly subscription.
