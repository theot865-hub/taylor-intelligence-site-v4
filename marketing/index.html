Questions

Why isn’t there one fixed price?+

A single campaign and a multi-channel content programme need different levels of work. We scope the channels, creative, content and ongoing management together, then quote the work. Advertising spend is set out separately from our fees.

Do we need to be on every platform?+

No. The right mix depends on your customers and your offer. We would rather start with a clear reason for a channel than add one just to fill a plan.

Can you help with the website as well?+

Yes. Website design, landing pages, marketing and software can be planned together, so the experience after the click is part of the work.
