Service 01

Consulting

The full picture, before you commit to a build.

Paid · Deep dive
What it is

A paid, comprehensive review of your business — every workflow, every tool, every place a machine could carry the load. Deeper than the free diagnostic: this is the one that produces a real plan.

What you get

A ranked, costed roadmap of the highest-ROI AI opportunities in your business — one document, yours to keep and act on, with us or without us.

Julian on Consulting
Transcript

Hi, I'm Julian from Taylor Intelligence. Most business owners know that AI can save them time, but they don't know what to automate, what it would cost, or if it would even make a meaningful difference at all. That's where our consultation comes in. We take a look at the work moving through your business — all of the missed calls, the leads that may go cold, or staff repeating the same repetitive tasks every day. We then create you a specific automated plan. We'll show you what we can improve, we'll filter out what doesn't need automation now, and we'll show you the impact that we can make. Leaving the call, you will have a better understanding of how AI can help you in your business — and that you won't have to deal with such technologies yourself, if we can handle them for you.

Approach

How we work.

Three services, one path. Every engagement starts with a free conversation and ends with a system you own. Here's the method — and exactly what each service does.

The method
01 — Diagnose

Find where AI pays off

Start free: a short conversation to spot the obvious wins. Want the full picture first? That's Consulting — a paid, deeper audit.

02 — Build

Put it into the business

Integration: we build the system on your accounts and run it. When it ships, you own it.

03 — Train

Hand over the keys

Education: your team learns to run it on their real work, so it keeps paying off after we leave.

One path, not a menu. Each stage's output is the next stage's starting point — nothing wasted, and every road leads to the build.

Integration

The build
What it is

Conduit — four parts
Front Desk

Answers every call and message, books the job, never misses after hours.

Chaser

Follows up on every lead and unpaid invoice, so nothing slips.

Memory

Remembers every customer, quote, and conversation — one brain for the business.

Dispatch

Routes the right job to the right person at the right time.

How it works

Built on your accounts and your numbers — nothing rented, nothing held hostage. When it ships, it's yours to keep running.
