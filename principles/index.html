Why Taylor Intelligence

Taylor is our co-founder Theo’s last name. It also nods to Taylorism: the idea that work can be studied, understood and improved.

Our approach starts with the people doing the work in your shop. We learn how enquiries become booked jobs, where follow-up gets missed and what slows your team down. AI, marketing and software should make that work easier.

Your technicians and service advisors bring the expertise and judgement. Technology can help with repeatable admin and customer follow-up, leaving your team more time for drivers and their vehicles.

The original idea: Taylor’s Principles of Scientific Management, 1911

Start with people

Good tools fit the people using them. We start with your service advisors, technicians and customers, and learn how your shop works before deciding what to build.

In practice: walk through a customer enquiry with your team.

Make it useful

A website should help a driver find the right service and contact your shop. An AI agent should have a clear job, such as organising enquiries or preparing follow-ups. We define the result each project should support.

In practice: connect each project to a task or result your shop values.

Keep it clear

You should understand what we’re building, what it costs and where your team stays involved. We make approval points clear for quotes, customer messages and other work that needs a person’s judgement.

In practice: agree the scope and explain the trade-offs.

Keep learning

Your shop changes with the season, your customers and the services you offer. We review how the work is performing and use feedback from your team to make the next improvement.

In practice: turn what we learn into the next useful improvement.
