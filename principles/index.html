Why Taylor Intelligence

Taylor is our co-founder Theo’s last name. It also nods to Taylorism: the idea that work can be studied, understood and improved.

Our approach today starts with the people doing the work. We learn how a business operates, find the repeated friction and build systems that make things clearer and easier. Software, AI and education are tools for that work.

People bring context and judgement. Technology can take on more of the repeatable tasks. We look for improvements that help both the business and its team.

The original idea: Taylor’s Principles of Scientific Management, 1911

Start with people

Good tools fit the people using them. We want to understand your customers, your team and the work they already do before we decide what to build.

In practice: start with a conversation about the business.

Make it useful

A beautiful website should help someone take the next step. An agent should have a clear job. We care about what the work does for your business as much as how it looks.

In practice: give each project a clear purpose.

Keep it clear

You should understand what we’re building, what it costs and where a person is still involved. Plain language and visible review points make better working relationships.

In practice: agree the scope and explain the trade-offs.

Keep learning

Technology changes. So do businesses. We build, try, review and teach along the way, so your team becomes more confident using the things we create.

In practice: turn what we learn into the next useful improvement.
