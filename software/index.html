How AgAAS works

AgAAS means Agentic as a Service: managed AI agents connected to the shop information and tools agreed for your workflow, with ongoing care from our team.

We start with one job at the service desk. The agent prepares or completes agreed steps and brings decisions back to your team. Integrations depend on the access your existing systems provide.

Give it a defined job

Start with incoming quote requests: identify the service, collect the vehicle details and prepare a reply for a service advisor to check.

Connect the right context

Use your shop’s opening hours, services and approved information. We agree what the agent can access and what needs a service advisor’s review.

Keep looking after it

Services, staff and shop systems change. We maintain the connections, instructions and review steps so the workflow stays useful.

A tire enquiry workflow

  1. A driver asks about tires

    A message arrives through an agreed channel, asking about tires or a seasonal changeover.

  2. The agent gathers details

    It organises the vehicle, tire size and service request, and flags missing information.

  3. A draft is prepared

    A reply is prepared using your approved shop information, with questions or next steps for the customer.

  4. Your service advisor decides

    Your team confirms fitment, pricing, availability and any booking before those details are promised.

Questions

How is this different from using a chatbot?+

An agent can connect a conversation to a defined shop task, such as organising a tire enquiry or preparing a service follow-up. It uses approved information and hands decisions back to your team where required.

What does it cost?+

Software and AgAAS are scoped individually. We agree on the workflow, available integrations, setup and ongoing support, then quote any build costs and recurring service fees clearly.
