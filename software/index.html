How AgAAS works

AgAAS is our shorthand for Agentic as a Service: AI agents connected to your business tools and information, with ongoing care from the people who built them.

We start with a defined job. The agent gets the context and access it needs, prepares or completes the agreed steps, and brings decisions back to a person where review is needed.

Give it a defined job

A useful first workflow might sort enquiries, gather research or prepare a reply. A clear start and finish makes it easier to see whether the work is helping.

Connect the right context

Your knowledge and tools give the workflow its context. We agree what it can access, what it can do and what needs your review.

Keep looking after it

Workflows change as the business changes. Ongoing maintenance keeps the connections, instructions and review points aligned with the job.

An enquiry workflow

  1. An enquiry arrives

    The workflow receives the message from an agreed source.

  2. The agent gathers context

    Relevant business information helps it understand the request.

  3. A draft is prepared

    The response and supporting context are organised for review.

  4. A person decides

    Your team approves the next step where the workflow requires it.

Questions

How is this different from using a chatbot?+

A chatbot is a place to have a conversation. A custom agent workflow can connect that reasoning to a defined task, your information and your tools. The useful part is the job it helps your team complete.

What does it cost?+

Software and AgAAS are scoped individually. The price depends on the workflow, integrations, access requirements and ongoing support. We’ll define the work together before quoting it.
