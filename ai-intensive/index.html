A few practical details

Before we build.

Who should be your internal AI person?+

Someone curious who understands the work and has time to practise between sessions. They can be an employee or the owner. You don’t need an existing AI expert; we match the workflow and tools to their starting point.

What kinds of workflow can we build?+

For example: drafting quotes, preparing customer follow-ups or finding answers in approved internal documents. We choose one achievable workflow together. Complex integrations and custom software development need a separate scope.

How are the sessions scheduled?+

We agree a start date and a weekly time together after the fit call. Sessions are private and held on Google Meet. The owner joins the kickoff and outcome review; most working time is with the nominated employee.

What does our business need to provide?+

A nominated employee, time between sessions, appropriate access and approved examples of the task. Your business pays any tool subscriptions directly. We agree the data and access needed before starting.
