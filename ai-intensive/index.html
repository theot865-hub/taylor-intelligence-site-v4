Who should be our internal AI person?+

Someone curious who knows how the business works and has time to practise between sessions. They can be an employee or the owner. They don’t need to be an AI expert; we choose the tools and workflow around their starting point.

What will we actually build?+

We agree one achievable workflow before starting. That could mean drafting quotes, preparing customer follow-ups or finding answers in approved internal documents. We build and test it together, add human review steps and write an operating guide. Custom software, complex integrations and extra workflows are scoped separately.

How do the three weeks work?+

We agree a start date and weekly time together. You get three private 90-minute working sessions on Google Meet, one each week, and two progress reviews between sessions. The owner joins the kickoff and outcome review; most working time is with the nominated employee.

What do we need to provide?+

One nominated employee, time between sessions, appropriate access and approved examples of the task. Your business pays any tool subscriptions directly. We agree the data and access needed before starting. The programme is $2,500 CAD per business; the initial fit call is free.
