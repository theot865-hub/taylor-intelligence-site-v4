A few questions

Before you decide.

Does the business owner attend?

The owner can attend, or send a team member. The price is $400 CAD for each guest. If you’re sending someone, agree on a useful task for them to bring.

Will they become an AI expert in a day?

The aim is a practical starting point: use AI on everyday work, check the results, and help colleagues get started. Specialist or complex projects can still need further support.

Do they need technical experience?

No coding experience is needed. Familiarity with the business and a willingness to practise matter more. We’ll confirm any accounts or tools needed before booking.

Should we bring confidential business information?

Use a non-sensitive example for the exercises. We’ll cover what to check before sharing information with an AI tool and where a person needs to review the result.

Does an enquiry book or charge me for a seat?

No. We’ll reply about availability, confirm the course details, and explain how to book. Sending this form does not reserve a seat or take payment.
